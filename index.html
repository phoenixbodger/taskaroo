How to Use Taskaroo

Getting Started

  • Everything runs in your browser. Taskaroo is a single-page app — no account or sign-in needed. Categories, notes, and tasks are managed right here, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • Data is saved automatically. Every change is stored in your browser's local storage, so your work persists between visits — but it stays on this browser/device. Use Backup & Restore to move it.
  • Where things live. The sidebar holds your categories and per-category notes; the main area shows tasks in Kanban or List view; the top bar has New Category, New Task, backup, and theme controls.

Categories

  • Create categories with the New Category button in the top bar, or hover a category row and use its + icon to add a sub-category — or a new task scoped to that category.
  • Nest them. Categories support sub-categories. Use the chevron to expand or collapse a branch, and the pencil to rename or re-parent a category.
  • Pin favourites. Click a category's star to pin it to the Favourites section at the top of the sidebar for quick access.
  • Category Notes. Select a single category and a notes box appears in the sidebar for specs, links, or reminders. Click Save Changes when you're done.
  • Filter by category. Tick a category's checkbox to show only its tasks; selecting a parent also selects its whole branch. Untick any category to hide it again, or click All Tasks to show everything. Deleting a category unassigns — but never deletes — its tasks.

Tasks

  • Create a task with the New Task button in the top bar, or hover a category row and use its + menu. A task carries a title, category, priority, status, optional due date/time, optional repeating rule, and optional subtasks.
  • Set a due date. Click the calendar button next to the field to open the picker (choose a date and time, then OK), or type directly like 2026-12-01 09:00. Badges show the due time — amber Due Today, red Overdue.
  • Priorities (High / Medium / Low) color-code cards and list rows so important work stands out.
  • Complete a task by ticking its checkbox (in Kanban or List view) or dragging the card onto the Done column. Untick or drag back to reopen it.
  • Edit or delete any task with the pencil / trash icons that appear on hover.

Subtasks & Checklists

  • Add steps while creating or editing a task: type into the Subtasks / Checklist box and press Enter or click Add.
  • Tick them off directly on the card. A progress bar and a 2/4-style counter show how much of the checklist is complete.

Kanban & List Views

  • Kanban shows three columns — To Do, In Progress, Done. Drag a card onto another column to move it; dropping on Done completes it.
  • List view shows every task in a sortable table. Click a column header to sort by task, category, priority, status, or due date (click again to reverse). Use the dropdowns to filter, and Clear filters to reset.
  • Switch anytime with the Kanban / List toggle above the columns; your choice is remembered.

Search & Filters

  • Search matches task titles and subtask titles — searching a step like "clean toilet bowl" finds its parent task.
  • Combine filters. Layer the priority dropdown, the category checkboxes, and the list view's own filters for a precise view.

Recurring Tasks

  • Set a rhythm. Tick Recurring in the task form and choose an interval — minute, hour, day, week, or month — plus an "every N" amount, for example every 2 weeks.
  • Limit repeats (optional). Enter how many additional completions to run after the first occurrence; leave the field blank (∞) to repeat forever.
  • On completion a recurring task returns to To Do with its next due date. Hourly/minute tasks skip missed occurrences and schedule from now; daily/weekly/monthly tasks roll forward, so a forgotten chore is still due next cycle.

Backup & Restore

  • Backup. The download (↓) icon in the top bar exports all of your data as a taskaroo_backup.json file you can keep anywhere.
  • Restore. The upload (↑) icon reads a backup and replaces the current data — handy when switching browsers or devices. An alert confirms success or warns about invalid files.
  • Everything stays private. Data lives in your browser's local storage, and JSON backups are the only files involved.
